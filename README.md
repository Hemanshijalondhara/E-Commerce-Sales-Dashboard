# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales Dashboard

An interactive **E-Commerce Sales Dashboard** developed using **Power BI and Excel** to analyze sales performance, revenue trends, product performance, and payment methods. The dashboard transforms raw e-commerce data into meaningful visual insights to support data-driven business decision-making.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The E-Commerce Sales Dashboard provides an interactive view of e-commerce business performance. It helps users analyze key sales metrics, identify revenue trends, compare product performance, and understand payment-method patterns.

The project focuses on converting raw sales data into clear **KPIs, interactive visualizations, and actionable business insights**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze overall e-commerce sales performance.
* Monitor important sales and revenue KPIs.
* Identify product-wise revenue trends.
* Analyze monthly sales and revenue patterns.
* Understand payment-method distribution.
* Identify trends and patterns that can support business decisions.
* Present complex sales data through an interactive dashboard.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualization
* **Excel** – Data preparation and analysis
* **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
* **DAX** – KPI and analytical calculations

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 🔹 KPI Analysis

The dashboard provides key performance indicators to monitor important business metrics such as:

* Quantity
* Average Price
* Minimum Price
* Maximum Price

### 🔹 Product Analysis

* Compare revenue across different products.
* Identify high-performing and low-performing products.
* Understand product contribution to overall revenue.

### 🔹 Monthly Trend Analysis

* Analyze revenue trends over time.
* Identify changes in monthly performance.
* Understand overall sales patterns.

### 🔹 Payment Method Analysis

* Analyze the distribution of different payment methods.
* Compare customer payment preferences.
* Understand payment-method patterns within the business.

### 🔹 Interactive Dashboard

Users can interact with the dashboard through visual filters and charts to explore different aspects of the sales data.
